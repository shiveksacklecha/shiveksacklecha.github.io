About

Trained in classical. At home on the big stage.

Shivek Sacklecha is a London-based, classically trained vocalist with over 10 years of experience in Indian classical and Bollywood music.

He performs across the UK at concerts, festivals, corporate events and private celebrations, bringing musical versatility, strong vocals and an engaging stage presence to every audience.
